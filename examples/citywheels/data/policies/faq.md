# Frequently asked questions

## When are you open?

- April to October: every day, 08:00–20:00.
- November to March: Monday to Saturday, 09:00–17:00. Closed on Sundays.
- Closed on 25 and 26 December and on 1 January.

Bikes can only be picked up and returned while the shop is open.

## Where are you?

At the main entrance of the Riverside Park, next to the tram stop "Park Nadrzeczny".

## What do I need to rent a bike?

An ID card or a passport, and a payment card or a voucher for the deposit. We do not take deposits in cash.

## How can I pay?

By card or with a CityWheels voucher, online or in the shop. In the shop you can also pay the rental in cash, but not the deposit.

## Are there age limits?

- Kids bikes: for children from 6 to 12 years old, rented by a parent or guardian.
- City bikes: from 13 years old. Riders under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign the rental.
- Electric bikes: from 16 years old. Riders under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign the rental.

## Do I have to wear a helmet?

Helmets are free and we recommend them for everyone. They are required for riders under 16.

## Can I take my dog?

Dogs are not allowed in bike baskets or child seats.

## Can I take the bike on a train or outside the city?

Yes, inside the country. Taking a bike abroad is not allowed.

## What if the bike breaks or I have an accident?

Stop riding, make sure you are safe, and call the staff at +48 000 000 000. Do not try to repair an electric bike yourself.

## What if the bike is stolen or lost?

Report it to the police and call the staff at once. The staff decide about the deposit after reading the police report.

## Do you sell bikes?

No. We only rent bikes.
