# Cancellations and refunds

## Cancelling a booking

A customer can cancel a booking with the status `confirmed`. How much of the rental price comes back depends on how long before the start of the rental the booking is cancelled:

| Cancelled | Refund of the rental price |
| --- | --- |
| 48 hours or more before the start | 100% |
| Less than 48 hours, but 24 hours or more before the start | 50% |
| Less than 24 hours before the start | 0% |

- The booking fee is never refunded.
- The deposit is always refunded in full when a booking is cancelled.
- Bookings with the status `in_progress`, `completed` or `cancelled` cannot be cancelled, and nothing is refunded for them.

## How the money comes back

- Bookings paid by card are refunded to the same card within 5 working days.
- Bookings paid with a voucher are refunded as a new voucher of the same value, never as money. The deposit is refunded the same way it was paid.

## Changing a booking

The dates or the bike type of a `confirmed` booking can be changed for free up to 24 hours before the start, if bikes are available. Later changes are not possible: the customer has to cancel and book again.

## Bad weather

If the weather service issues a storm warning for the day of the rental, the customer may get a full refund. The staff decide each case. Customers have to contact the staff for this.
