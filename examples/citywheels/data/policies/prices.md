# Prices

All prices are in euros (EUR) and include VAT.

## Bike rental

| Bike type | Per hour | Per day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City bike (`city`) | 4 EUR | 20 EUR |
| Electric bike (`e-bike`) | 9 EUR | 40 EUR |
| Kids bike (`kids`) | 2 EUR | 10 EUR |

- Hourly rentals are charged for every started hour.
- A day is up to 24 hours from the start of the rental. A rental longer than 6 hours is charged at the day price.
- Rentals of 7 days or more get 15% off the rental price.

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Child seat (`child_seat`) | 3 EUR per day |
| Extra lock | Free |
| Helmet | Free |

## Booking fee

Bookings made online have a booking fee of 2 EUR per booking. Bookings made in the shop have no booking fee.

## Deposit

Every bike needs a deposit, paid with the booking:

| Bike type | Deposit per bike |
| --- | --- |
| City bike | 50 EUR |
| Electric bike | 150 EUR |
| Kids bike | 30 EUR |

The deposit is returned in full when the bike comes back on time and without damage. Damage is checked by the staff when the bike is returned.

## Late return

A bike returned after the end of the rental is charged 1.5 times the hourly price for every started hour of delay. The fee is taken from the deposit.
